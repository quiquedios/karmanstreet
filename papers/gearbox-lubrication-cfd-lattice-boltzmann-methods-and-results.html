Gearbox lubrication CFD (Lattice Boltzmann): methods and results

Paper · Project 42

This paper documents a CFD study of oil–air multiphase flow inside a gearbox, carried out with a GPU-accelerated Lattice Boltzmann Method solver to quantify lubricant distribution and torque losses under realistic operating conditions.

Method

The model resolved the interaction between two rotating gears at a 1.54:1 transmission ratio, tracking the oil–air interface to measure impregnation and wetted area, and recording the instantaneous torque on each gear. A mesh-sensitivity study compared a 40k-element grid against a 324k-element grid.

Results

Wetted surface area settled at 32.5% on the driving gear and 56% on the driven gear, with quasi-periodic torque losses. The two mesh resolutions diverged significantly, indicating that finer lattice refinement and smaller time steps are needed for a converged answer — the study's main methodological finding.
