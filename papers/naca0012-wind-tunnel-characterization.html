NACA0012 wind-tunnel characterization

Paper · Project 63

This paper reports experimental aerodynamic testing of a NACA 0012 aerofoil in a wind tunnel, combining flow visualisation with surface-pressure measurement across angle of attack.

Method

Tracer dynamics and flow-fluctuation frequencies were analysed to confirm tracer suitability (57 kHz cutoff against 83 Hz flow) and to assess buoyancy effects on the visualisation. Surface-pressure taps were processed into pressure and lift coefficients, with a full uncertainty analysis.

Results

The lift curve was linear to 8–10° before stall onset, consistent with theory. Lift and drag per unit span were quantified (L′ = 5.35–7.61 N/m, D′ = 0.32–0.42 N/m), flow separation was studied through both pressure data and visualisation, and relative uncertainties stayed below 1% across most operating conditions.
