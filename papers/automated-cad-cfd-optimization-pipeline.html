Automated CAD–CFD optimization pipeline

Paper · Master's thesis, Ch. 4

This paper describes the automated CAD–CFD pipeline at the heart of the F1 front-wing thesis: the machinery that lets an expensive, geometry-heavy optimisation run without a human in the loop.

Parametric CAD

The wing is fully parametrised in CATIA V5 and driven programmatically through its scripting interface. A Python routine reads the active parameters and their physical ranges from a spreadsheet, draws a Latin Hypercube of designs over the resulting high-dimensional cube, and for each sample writes the parameters into the CATIA template, regenerates the geometry and exports it — twice per design, once for each aerodynamic mode.

Automated CFD reduction

Once meshed and solved, each run's force-coefficient history is reduced automatically: a routine reads the exported CSVs, finds where the oscillating signal settles within a ±2% band, and averages the tail to a single Cl or Cd value written straight back to the design database — leaving the surrogate ready to train. The result is a reproducible, seed-controlled pipeline from parameters to coefficients.
