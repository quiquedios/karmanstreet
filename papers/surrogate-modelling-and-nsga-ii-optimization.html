Surrogate modelling and NSGA-II optimization

Paper · Master's thesis, Ch. 5

This paper covers the data-driven core of the thesis: fitting a surrogate to a limited set of CFD runs and using it to find the optimal wing designs.

Surrogate and active learning

A Gaussian-process surrogate was trained on 70×2 Latin-Hypercube designs — about 2.7 samples per dimension over the 26-dimensional space — then improved by active learning, with parity plots and forward-prediction checks tracking its accuracy across batches until it was trustworthy enough to freeze.

Multi-objective optimisation

The frozen surrogate was searched with an NSGA-II genetic algorithm to produce a Pareto front of downforce-versus-drag optima. Specific operating points were then selected per circuit using the fraction of each 2025 F1 lap spent with DRS open, turning the front into concrete, track-specific geometries.
