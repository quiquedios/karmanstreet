Impinging-jet heat transfer via IR thermography

Paper · Project 64

This paper presents an experimental study of convective heat transfer under an impinging jet (Re = 10,000), using non-intrusive infrared thermography to obtain spatially resolved temperature fields.

Method

A MATLAB processing pipeline computed the local heat-transfer coefficient field h(x,y) and Nusselt-number distributions from an energy balance that accounted for Joule heating, radiation losses and in-plane conduction, at 0.08 mm/pixel resolution with roughly 5% uncertainty.

Results

Increasing the jet-to-plate distance (H/D = 2–6) raised the heat-transfer coefficient (h = 100→120 W/m²K, Nu = 45→55), consistent with jet-development theory. A non-axisymmetric rectangular nozzle enhanced peak heat transfer to about 127 W/m²K, and radial Nusselt profiles showed the expected stagnation peak and wall-jet decay.
