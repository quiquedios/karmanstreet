Hot-wire anemometry and cylinder-wake analysis

Paper · Project 65

This paper covers experimental aerodynamic measurement with hot-wire anemometry: full sensor characterization, calibration, and analysis of a circular cylinder wake.

Sensor characterisation

The probe's dynamic response was determined through impulse analysis, obtaining a cutoff frequency of 10 kHz and confirming suitability for resolving the turbulent fluctuations of interest.

Calibration used King's Law over a velocity range of 3–20 m/s:

E² = A + B · Un with A = 2.336, B = 2.127, n = 0.4628

achieving a fit accuracy of R² = 0.9998, which enables reliable conversion of voltage signals into instantaneous velocity.

Wake analysis

The wake of a circular cylinder of D = 32 mm was analysed using high-frequency sampling, computing power spectral densities to identify vortex shedding frequencies and their dependence on freestream velocity.

Results

Turbulence statistics revealed significant velocity deficits and high turbulence intensity of 27–32% in the wake. Time-frequency analysis via STFT tracked the shedding frequency evolution, confirming temporal stability and agreement with Strouhal-based theoretical predictions.
