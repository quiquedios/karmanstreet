CFD–wind-tunnel correlation of a scale model

Paper · Master's thesis, Ch. 4 & 6

This paper closes the loop on the thesis by comparing the CFD predictions against physical measurement, and by dissecting the flow field of the optimal designs.

Scale-model testing

A 32.4%-scale model of the wing was divided into printable sections, 3-D printed and assembled, then tested in a wind tunnel — with the setup and preliminary checks documented — to provide an experimental point of correlation for the simulations.

Flow-field study

For the selected optima the paper examines chordwise pressure coefficient at mid-span, wall-y+ maps on both surfaces, surface Cp from above and below, and — on a plane behind the wing — the streamwise vorticity, total-pressure loss and turbulent kinetic energy, explaining how the geometry produces its downforce and where its losses come from.
