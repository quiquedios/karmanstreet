About

Hi! I'm Enrique. I am an aeronautical and motorsports engineer who's obsessed with aerodynamics, and I wanted to register and share all my thoughts, ideas and projects. That is what this website is for.

I believe the most advanced aerodynamics are found in military aircraft and in Formula 1. Military aircraft, such as fighter jets, push their operational limits way further than civil aircraft, which is logical. Formula 1, on the other hand, operates in a subsonic regime, but the stakes depending on the most slight aerodynamic details are huge. This being said, I currently work as an aerodynamics & CFD engineer in defence for an Airbus aircraft, and F1 aerodynamicist remains my dream job.
