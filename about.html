About

Hi — I'm Enrique Dios Lázaro, an aerodynamics and CFD engineer. This site is where I explain how air behaves in plain terms, write up the aerodynamics and CFD work I do, and keep a running portfolio.

The name is a nod to the von Kármán vortex street: the regular, alternating pattern of swirls that peels off a bluff body in a flow. It felt like a fitting name for a place that's mostly about what air does.

My work spans external and internal aerodynamics, high-fidelity CFD, and aeroelasticity — from airfoil and wing analysis to full-vehicle and engine-inlet simulations, usually paired with a good deal of automation and validation against experiment.

Tools I work with

CFD solvers: Ansys Fluent, STAR-CCM+, SU2, XFlow, OpenFOAM, SimScale.
CAD: CATIA V5, SolidWorks.
Automation & analysis: Python, MATLAB & Simulink, ParaView.
