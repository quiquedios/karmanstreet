Aeromap and aerodynamic balance

Foundations · outline to complete

The final applied chapter connects aerodynamics to the rest of the car. A wing does not produce a single number but a map of coefficients across ride heights and attitudes, and how that map is shaped — especially its balance front to rear — is where aerodynamics meets vehicle dynamics.

To write — sources to merge:
  • The aeromap — downforce and balance as functions of front/rear ride height; how it is built and used. MSI aerodynamics notes §12 (Aeromap).
  • Aerodynamic balance and centre of pressure — front-to-rear load split and its effect on handling. Milliken §15.12 (Aerodynamics and Balance); MSI §3 (Aerodynamics' influence on performance).
  • Aero and vehicle performance — downforce vs drag vs lap time; the tyre-load sensitivity that makes downforce worth its drag. Milliken Ch. 15 and the vehicle-performance chapters; Katz Ch. 5 (Aerodynamics and Vehicle Performance).
This chapter is the bridge out of pure aerodynamics into vehicle dynamics, so it should stay at the level of how the aeromap is produced and what balance means, and hand off to the dynamics sources rather than attempting to cover them.
