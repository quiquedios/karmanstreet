Theory

The theoretical foundations behind my work, drawn from my Master's thesis. Four chapters, read in order or on their own.

  • 1. On aerodynamics

    Aerodynamic forces, lift and drag generation, and wings in motorsport.

  • 2. On CFD

    Navier–Stokes and RANS, turbulence models, wall treatment and discretisation.

  • 3. On wind tunnels

    Dynamic similarity, blockage, wall interference and boundary-layer tripping.

  • 4. On DOE and optimization

    Sampling, surrogate models, Bayesian optimization and genetic algorithms.
