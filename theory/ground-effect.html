Ground effect

Foundations · outline to complete

Ground effect is introduced alongside multi-element wings in the previous chapter, since the front wing lives in it permanently. This chapter treats it in its own right — as the mechanism that the modern underbody is built around — and connects it to the underbody and diffuser chapter that follows.

To write — sources to merge:
  • The mechanism — a downforce surface near the ground as a converging channel; the suction peak; the downforce-vs-ride-height curve up to the critical clearance where it collapses. Already covered from the wing side in Ground effect and multi-element wings; expand here with the underbody view. McBeath Ch. 6 (Underbody aerodynamics); Katz Ch. 6 (Sliding Seals and Skirts, Underbody Channels).
  • Ground effect without skirts — how modern regulations recover it. Milliken, Race Car Vehicle Dynamics, §15.5–15.6 (Ground Effects / Ground Effects Without Skirts).
  • Historical development — the Lotus 78/79 lineage and why skirts were banned. Milliken §15.1; McBeath Ch. 1.
