Design of Experiments and Latin Hypercube Sampling

Theory · Thesis §2.1.4

When each evaluation of a design is expensive — a full CFD run, say — you cannot afford to sample the design space carelessly. Design of Experiments (DOE) is the discipline of choosing where to sample so that a limited budget of runs teaches you as much as possible.

Why not a grid?

A full factorial grid explodes combinatorially with the number of variables, and pure random sampling tends to clump and leave gaps. Neither is efficient in high dimensions.

Latin Hypercube Sampling

Latin Hypercube Sampling (LHS) divides each variable's range into equal bins and draws samples so that every bin of every variable is used exactly once. The result is a well-spread, space-filling set of designs that covers the space evenly with far fewer points than a grid. In the thesis, LHS seeds the initial training set for the surrogate model over a 26-dimensional design space.
