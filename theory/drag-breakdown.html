Drag breakdown

Theory · Thesis §2.1.1

Drag is not a single mechanism. It is useful to separate it first by its physical origin, then by its cause.

Skin friction vs pressure drag

Skin-friction drag comes from shear stress in the boundary layer — the air rubbing along the surface. Pressure drag comes from the fore-aft imbalance of pressure, which grows dramatically once the flow separates and leaves a low-pressure wake. A streamlined shape is mostly skin friction; a bluff body is mostly pressure drag.

Form drag vs induced drag

Pressure drag can be split again. Form drag depends on the shape and how it sheds its wake. Induced drag is the unavoidable price of producing lift on a finite wing: the tip vortices tilt the local lift vector backward, and that rearward component is drag. Induced drag falls as aspect ratio rises, which is why efficient wings are long and slender.
