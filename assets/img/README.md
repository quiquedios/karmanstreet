Drop your figures here, matching the paths shown in the dashed placeholder boxes
on each page.

  assets/img/theory/    figures for the theory posts
  assets/img/projects/  figures for the project pages
  assets/img/papers/    figures for the paper pages

To replace a placeholder, swap this block in the page:

  <div class="fig-box">Description<code>/assets/img/.../name.png</code></div>

for:

  <img src="/assets/img/.../name.png" alt="Description">

The surrounding <figure> and <figcaption> stay as they are.
