2024 Formula 1 car parametric CFD

Project · Project 46

A full parametric aerodynamic design methodology for a 2024-specification Formula 1 car, integrating CAD-based geometry generation in CATIA V5 with high-fidelity CFD validation under FIA regulatory constraints.

The car was built from first principles using FIA control volumes, enabling rapid iteration of the front wing (multi-element parametric profiles), the floor with its strakes and diffuser, the rear wing with beam-wing coupling, and full bodywork integration. CFD used a GPU solver (XFlow) in a 98×57×57 m tunnel domain at 50 m/s with near-wall refinement to 4 mm.

From the solution I analysed the flow structures — velocity contours, pressure distributions and vorticity fields — identifying the key phenomena of wake upwash, underfloor acceleration and pressure recovery in the diffuser.
