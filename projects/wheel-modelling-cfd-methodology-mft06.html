Wheel-modelling CFD methodology (MFT06)

Project · Project 72

A methodology study assessing how wheel-geometry simplification and mesh parameters affect aerodynamic accuracy and cost for the MFT06 Formula Student car — the kind of groundwork that makes a full-vehicle CFD campaign affordable.

I built wheel models at several fidelity levels (full rim, simplified rim with spokes, closed rim) and measured their influence on aerodynamic coefficients and wake structure under constrained resources, isolating the front axle first and later extending to full-vehicle runs using symmetry to cut cost. Simplified geometries proved usable within acceptable deviations, provided the extra recirculation of a closed rim was accounted for.

A comprehensive mesh-sensitivity study varied refinement, boundary-layer resolution and growth rate, judging convergence through residuals, y+, non-orthogonality and the aerodynamic coefficients — establishing local mesh independence and selecting a configuration that balanced accuracy against compute.
