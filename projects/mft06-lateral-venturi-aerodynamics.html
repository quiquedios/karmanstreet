MFT06 lateral Venturi aerodynamics

Project · Project 71

Aerodynamic development of the lateral Venturi system for the MFT06 Formula Student car, combining CAD in SolidWorks with extensive parametric CFD in SimScale across a wide range of geometric configurations.

I developed and evaluated multiple concepts — cascade elements at varying angle of attack and chord, aerofoil profiles, wheelplates, strakes and nose-wing configurations — exploring a large design space through structured iteration. Flow analysis via velocity contours and aerodynamic coefficients (Cl, Cd) guided the decisions, focusing on flow attachment, separation control and downstream conditioning.

The Venturi was optimised under regulatory and packaging constraints, including internal component integration, improving flow quality and downforce while holding drag. Critical issues such as recirculation and separation were resolved through geometry and aerofoil refinement, with low-impact concepts discarded on a performance-versus-complexity basis.
