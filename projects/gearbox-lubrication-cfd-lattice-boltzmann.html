Gearbox lubrication CFD (Lattice Boltzmann)

Project · Project 42

A CFD study of oil–air multiphase flow inside a gearbox, using a GPU-accelerated Lattice Boltzmann Method solver to analyse lubricant distribution and torque losses under realistic operating conditions.

The model captured the fluid interaction between rotating gears at a 1.54:1 transmission ratio, quantifying oil impregnation and the wetted surface area — 32.5% on the driving gear, 56% on the driven gear — along with the quasi-periodic torque losses. A mesh-sensitivity study (40k vs 324k elements) exposed significant discrepancies between resolutions, pointing to the need for finer lattice refinement and smaller time steps to reach convergence.

The full methodology and results are written up in the accompanying paper.
