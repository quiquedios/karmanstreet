Surrogate-based Bayesian optimization of an F1 front wing

Project · Master's thesis

My Master's thesis: an end-to-end aerodynamic design and optimisation pipeline for a parametrised Formula 1 front wing and nose assembly compliant with the 2026 FIA regulations, integrating CAD automation, CFD and data-driven optimisation.

The geometry is fully parametrised in CATIA V5 and driven from Python, so designs regenerate automatically across a 26-dimensional parameter space. A Latin Hypercube of designs seeds a Gaussian-process surrogate, which is refined by active learning and then frozen and searched with an NSGA-II genetic algorithm to produce a Pareto front of downforce-versus-drag optima — from which specific geometries are chosen per circuit using each track's DRS time fraction. High-resolution CFD in OpenFOAM and wind-tunnel testing of a 32.4%-scale 3D-printed model close the loop with experimental validation.
