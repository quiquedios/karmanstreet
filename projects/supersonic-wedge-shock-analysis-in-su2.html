Supersonic wedge shock analysis in SU2

Project · Project 14

A high-fidelity CFD study of supersonic inviscid flow over a 2-D wedge, solving the Euler equations to capture oblique shock formation and propagation in a structured domain. The solver was SU2, with post-processing in ParaView.

The core of the study was numerical: I compared spatial discretisation at first and second order and evaluated several flux schemes — HLLC, JST, Lax-Friedrichs, CUSP and Roe — for their effect on shock resolution, numerical diffusion and stability. Higher-order schemes sharpened the shock; lower-order ones smeared it with added diffusion.

Quantitative Mach-number profiles along specified lines pinned down shock location and strength across the configurations, turning a textbook flow into a concrete comparison of scheme behaviour.
