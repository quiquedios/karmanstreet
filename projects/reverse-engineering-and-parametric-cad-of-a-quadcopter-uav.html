Reverse engineering and parametric CAD of a quadcopter UAV

Project · Project 6

A full reverse-engineering exercise: measuring a commercial quadcopter and rebuilding it as a complete digital twin. The finished model comprised 98 individual components — 41 of them unique — organised into 9 functional subassemblies covering propulsion, chassis, battery and control systems.

The work involved precise dimensional measurement of the physical hardware and translation into fully constrained parametric CAD, holding tolerances down to 0.005 mm and one degree on critical features to guarantee geometric fidelity and assembly compatibility. I produced detailed technical drawings for every component — gears, structural arms, propellers, electronic housings — and validated the whole assembly hierarchically, capturing the mechanical interfaces and the motion path from motors to propellers.

Alongside the geometry I analysed the UAV's functional architecture — power distribution, propulsion mechanics and structural layout — linking each design decision back to performance and operational constraints.
