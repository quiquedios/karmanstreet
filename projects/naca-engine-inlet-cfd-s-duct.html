NACA engine inlet CFD (S-duct)

Project · Project 17

A comprehensive CFD study of a NACA-type engine inlet in STAR-CCM+, evaluating total-pressure recovery, compressor-face distortion and mass-flow efficiency across angles of attack of 0°, 15° and 30°. The flow was compressible and turbulent (Mach 0.21) through an S-duct geometry.

Setting it up meant a full physics definition — RANS with both k-ε and k-ω SST closures, boundary conditions and ideal-gas properties — plus custom field functions to compute pressure recovery and distortion directly from the solution. The mesh used trimmed cells and prism layers with quality validation (skewness, aspect ratio, y+) and refinement studies for numerical stability.

Results showed inlet performance degrading with angle of attack — falling pressure recovery and rising distortion at the compressor face — consistent with expectations, and a turbulence-model comparison in which k-ω SST agreed best with experiment.
