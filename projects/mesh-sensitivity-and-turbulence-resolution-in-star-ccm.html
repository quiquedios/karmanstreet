Mesh sensitivity and turbulence resolution in STAR-CCM+

Project · Project 15

A detailed CFD study of subsonic flow (Mach 0.42, Re = 10⁶) over a bluff body in STAR-CCM+, focused on mesh sensitivity and near-wall turbulence resolution through systematic variation of the prismatic boundary-layer discretisation.

I generated polyhedral meshes with increasing boundary-layer refinement — from 1 to 6 prism layers — and tracked their effect on wall-resolved y+, aerodynamic force coefficients and convergence. Adding layers cut y+ from 433 down to 79, sharpening the near-wall resolution, while the force coefficients converged toward a stable 2.5×10⁻³, confirming mesh independence.

A parallel-performance study across 2 to 8 cores and several mesh sizes exposed the non-linear scaling of cost with resolution, framing the practical trade-off between accuracy and compute.
